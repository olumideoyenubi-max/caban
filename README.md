# Storm Island

A Fortnite-style battle royale that runs in the browser. One HTML file, built with Three.js.

Open `index.html` in a browser (or serve the folder with any static server) and press **Drop in**.

## What's in it

- **Bus drop**: ride the flying bus across the island and jump whenever you like. Skydive, then a glider opens near the ground.
- **Bot lobby**: you against 10, 24 or 39 bots (Easy, Normal or Hard). They loot, fight each other, rotate with the storm, chase supply drops and build walls when shot at.
- **Building**: walls, floors and ramps on a 4 m grid, 10 wood each. Hold fire while walking to ramp-rush.
- **Harvesting**: swing the pickaxe at trees and rocks for wood.
- **Weapons**: pickaxe, assault rifle, pump shotgun, rapid SMG, bolt sniper (scoped) and a rocket launcher whose blast wrecks builds. Headshots deal bonus damage.
- **Rarity**: weapons come in Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary. Higher tiers hit harder, and picking up a better copy upgrades yours.
- **Healing and gear**: bandages (up to 75 health), med kits, shield potions and launch pads that throw you about 40 m into the air.
- **Loot**: gold chests, glowing loot on the ground in every town, and loot dropped by eliminated bots.
- **Supply drops**: crates parachute into the next safe zone during the storm and hold legendary loot.
- **Storm**: six phases that shrink toward a random safe circle and hurt more each phase.
- **HUD**: minimap with storm, next circle and supply drops; a full-screen map; kill feed; health and shield; hotbar with rarity colors; item bar; ammo; damage numbers; hit markers; red arcs showing where you're being shot from.
- **End screen**: placement, eliminations, time survived, damage dealt and builds placed. A win shows confetti.
- **Settings** for bot count, difficulty and aim sensitivity are remembered in your browser.
- **Touch controls** on phones and tablets.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD | Move |
| Mouse | Aim (click the game to lock the cursor) |
| Left click | Fire / place a build piece |
| Right click | Aim down sights |
| Space | Jump / leave the bus |
| Shift | Sprint |
| 1–6 | Pickaxe, AR, shotgun, SMG, sniper, rocket launcher |
| Q / F / C | Build wall / floor / ramp (press again to put away) |
| R | Reload |
| E | Open chest |
| 7 / 8 / 9 | Bandages / med kit / shield potion |
| H | Quick heal (picks the best item) |
| 0 | Place a launch pad |
| M | Full map |
